How to use voice

  • Press Speak, then say “from Cubbon Park to MG Road”.
  • Say “directions to Church Street” to start from your location.
  • Say “next” or “back” to move through the steps one at a time.
  • Say “read again” or “stop”.
  • Shortcuts: Alt+M speak, Alt+R read again, Alt+N / Alt+P next / previous step, Esc stop.
